# Goals and Non-Goals

> **Status:** Draft · **Date:** May 2026

---

## Goals

- Let developers deploy containerised apps with nothing more than a git push
- Support HTTP servers, background workers, and scheduled jobs
- Provide managed persistence: blob storage, databases, and queues
- Enforce cost caps automatically via quota limits — no manual intervention for routine use
- Keep apps unreachable from the public internet when required
- Require part-time attention from a single, rotating operator

---

## Non-Goals

- Production workloads.
- Multi-region or high-availability deployments
- Workloads requiring GPUs or specialised hardware
